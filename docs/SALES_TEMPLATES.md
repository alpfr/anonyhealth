# Sales Playbook & Cold Outreach Templates

## 🎯 Target Persona: Digital Health Officers / Innovation Directors
**Primary Pain Points:** Telehealth abandonment rates, patient privacy concerns, routing inefficiencies in digital clinics.
**Value Prop:** A scalable, compliant, zero-friction AI front door.

### Subject Line Ideas:
- Fixing the telemedicine "abandoned cart"
- A privacy-first digital front door for [Health System Name]
- Maryland telehealth compliance + AI Triage
- Are patients bouncing from your intake portal?
- Quick question about [Health System Name]'s digital triage
- Anonymous symptom checking for your patients

---

## 📧 Cold Email Template 1: The "Digital Front Door" (Focus on Routing)
**Subject:** Fixing the telemedicine "abandoned cart" at [Health System Name]

**Body:**
Hi [Name],

One of the biggest leaks we're seeing in digital healthcare systems right now is top-of-funnel abandonment. Patients land on a telehealth portal with sensitive symptoms, are met with an intimidating 5-page identity intake form, and they bounce before ever seeing a doctor.

We built AnonyHealth to fix that. It’s an interactive, privacy-first mobile prototype that flips the intake process. 

Our native AI handles an empathetic, anonymous symptom check directly on the edge. It triages the severity, matches the patient to a provider in your specific directory, and only initiates the heavy identity/compliance verification as the final step when trust is established.

It securely bridges the gap between hesitant patients and critical care pathways.

I’d love to show you a quick 5-minute demo of the triage logic in action. Are you open to a brief chat next Tuesday?

Best,

[Your Name]
AnonyHealth

## 📧 Cold Email Template 2: The "Compliance Walled Garden" (Focus on Security)
**Subject:** Privacy-first digital front door for [Health System Name]

**Body:**
Hi [Name],

Balancing patient privacy with state telehealth regulations (like mandatory identity verification and address capture) is a constant headache when deploying new AI chat applications.

We engineered AnonyHealth to solve this. It’s a complete React/Supabase stack built specifically around physical data isolation. When a patient uses our AI triage agent, their clinical symptom data is completely severed from their Personal Identifiable Information (PII) at the database layer (Supabase RLS). 

We've achieved the perfect balance: an anonymous, frictionless AI chat environment for the patient, alongside strict compliance for the facility. 

Can I send over a quick 2-minute workflow diagram showing how we isolate PII during a mock triage session?

Best,

[Your Name]
AnonyHealth
